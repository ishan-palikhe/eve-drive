MicroStation drawing viewer for mobile
DGN V7 & V8 · MOBILE

Open CAD drawings
on your phone.

View MicroStation drawings on your Galaxy without installing desktop CAD software.

Open DGN Viewer ↗

Tap above, then choose your .dgn file from Downloads or your phone storage in the viewer.

🔎 Zoom & panPinch to zoom, drag to move around the drawing.
📏 MeasureMeasure distances in the drawing units.
☷ LevelsToggle levels on and off to isolate details.
📤 ExportSave views as PNG or PDF and export DXF.

How to use

1
Tap Open DGN Viewer.
2
Tap Choose DGN File and select a drawing from your phone.
3
Use pinch zoom and drag to navigate; use the viewer tools to measure or export.

The DGN viewer processes drawings in your browser. Eve Drive is a launcher for the viewer engine; drawings are not sent to Eve Drive.
